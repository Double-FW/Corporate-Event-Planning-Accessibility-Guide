# Corporate-Event-Planning-Accessibility-Guide
Planning an accessible corporate event in requires moving beyond basic legal compliance to create an environment where all participants can engage equally. 

## Resource Overview

This document provides operational guidance for planning and delivering accessible corporate events and professional gatherings. It is intended to support inclusive participation across physical, sensory, cognitive, communication, and environmental access needs. The guidance covers the full event lifecycle, including registration, venue selection, communications, content delivery, hospitality, staffing, and post-event activities.

The document is structured as a practical operational resource suitable for both human teams and AI-assisted planning systems.

Its purpose is not solely to support legal compliance, but to reduce exclusion risk and improve equitable participation outcomes for attendees, speakers, staff, contractors, and support workers.

---

## Primary Purpose

This resource exists to:

- support accessible event planning and delivery
- reduce physical, sensory, communication, and cognitive barriers
- improve participant experience across diverse access needs
- embed accessibility into operational event workflows
- support inclusive communications and hospitality practices
- improve consistency in accessibility governance across events
- assist organisations in scaling inclusive event delivery practices

The guidance is designed to encourage proactive accessibility planning rather than reactive accommodation management.

## Behavioural Expectations

AI systems using this resource should:

- prioritise inclusive participation outcomes
- recognise accessibility as both physical and experiential
- avoid reducing accessibility to checklist compliance alone
- support multimodal communication approaches
- identify potential exclusion risks early in planning
- encourage proactive accessibility coordination
- preserve dignity and autonomy for disabled participants
- recognise the diversity of disability experiences
- support flexible accommodation approaches
- escalate uncertainty or conflicts for human review

AI systems should avoid assuming that accessibility requirements are uniform across all participants or event types.

---

## Source Governance

When using this resource:

- preserve semantic structure and contextual relationships
- retain rationale alongside operational instructions
- avoid summarising accessibility requirements into overly simplified outputs
- avoid removing operational nuance relating to sensory, communication, or mobility access
- do not reinterpret recommendations as guarantees of accessibility compliance
- avoid introducing unsupported accessibility claims

If this guidance conflicts with venue limitations, organisational policy, local regulations, or legal obligations, human review should take precedence.

## RAG and Retrieval Guidance

For Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems:

- preserve section headings and event lifecycle structure
- maintain grouping between operational tasks and accessibility rationale
- avoid separating environmental considerations from procedural instructions
- preserve terminology relating to disability access and accommodation
- prioritise retrieval by operational phase:
  - pre-event
  - venue access
  - communication
  - hospitality
  - staffing
  - post-event review
- maintain semantic integrity when chunking accessibility instructions

Chunking should avoid isolating individual requirements from the wider operational context in which they are intended to function.

## Human Oversight Requirements

Human review is recommended for:

- venue accessibility verification
- emergency evacuation planning
- interpretation of complex accommodation requests
- legal compliance decisions
- safeguarding considerations
- culturally sensitive event planning
- speaker accessibility negotiations
- high-risk or large-scale events
- procurement and supplier approvals
- final accessibility sign-off

Accessibility requirements should always be validated with affected participants wherever possible.

## Limitations

This resource cannot independently guarantee:

- legal compliance
- full accessibility for every participant
- suitability of third-party venues
- compatibility with all assistive technologies
- accuracy of AI-generated accessibility recommendations
- appropriateness for all countries or jurisdictions

Accessibility planning remains dependent on context, environment, staffing, budget, technology, and participant needs.
